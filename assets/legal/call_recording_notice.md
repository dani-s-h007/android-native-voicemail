# Call Recording & Caller Consent Notice

**Jurisdiction:** Republic of India  
**Governing Laws:** Indian Telegraph Act (1885), Information Technology Act (2000), Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) directives.

## 1. Statutory Notice to Callers
Under Indian law, automated recording of voice calls requires transparent notice to the calling party. Vani Voicemail enforces this requirement through the following safeguards:
1. **Mandatory Audible Greeting:** Before any voicemail recording starts or audio is processed, the caller is played an unambiguous automated prompt (e.g. *"Hi, you have reached Danish K. I am currently unavailable. Please leave a message or send a WhatsApp message"*).
2. **Explicit Disconnect Option:** Any caller who does not wish to have their message recorded may hang up immediately during or following the greeting prompt.

## 2. No Secret or Covert Recording
Vani Voicemail does NOT perform stealth or covert recording of active two-party phone conversations. It operates strictly as a visual voicemail and call screening assistant for unattended or dismissed calls.

## 3. Law Enforcement & Emergency Services
Emergency numbers (`112`, `100`, `108`, `101`) are permanently locked and will NEVER be deflected, recorded, or intercepted by the assistant.
