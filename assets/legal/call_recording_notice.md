## 1. Statutory Notice to Callers
Under applicable telecommunications regulations, recording voice messages requires transparent notice to the calling party:
1. **Audible Greeting Prompt:** Before any audio recording or voicemail processing begins, callers hear an automated audible greeting clearly notifying them that they have reached your voicemail assistant (e.g., *"Hello, you have reached {{APP_NAME}}. Please leave a message after the tone"*).
2. **Explicit Opportunity to Disconnect:** Any caller who does not consent to leave a recording may hang up immediately before or during the prompt.

## 2. Prohibition of Covert Recording
{{APP_NAME}} does NOT conduct stealth, secret, or covert recording of active telephone conversations. It functions strictly as an automated call assistant and visual voicemail receiver for missed, busy, or screened calls.

## 3. Mandatory Emergency Services Exclusion
National emergency dispatch numbers are permanently locked in the system and are never intercepted, deflected, or screened.
