# Legal Disclaimer & Operational Scope

**Product:** Vani Voicemail  
**Developer:** Danish K ([https://danishk.web.app](https://danishk.web.app))

## 1. Carrier Telephony Interactions
Vani Voicemail interacts with Indian cellular networks (Jio, Airtel, Vodafone Idea, BSNL) using standard 3GPP and GSM supplementary service codes (`*403*`, `*67*`, `*402*`, `*61*`).
- The developer is not an official telecommunications carrier and does not bill users for cellular network traffic.
- Users are advised to review their carrier's tariff plans regarding call forwarding and voicemail pulse billing.

## 2. Emergency Services
Do not rely on this application for life-critical or emergency dispatch communications. Emergency numbers (`112`, `100`, `108`) are configured to automatically bypass screening.

## 3. Transcription Accuracy
Machine transcription provided by on-device or neural speech engines is offered as an assistive convenience. Accuracy may vary based on cellular audio quality, background noise, regional dialects, or multilingual speech.
