## 1. Zero Permanent Cloud Storage Commitment
{{APP_NAME}} operates under an uncompromising **Zero Permanent Cloud Storage Architecture**. We believe your private phone calls, voice messages, transcripts, and conversations belong exclusively to you.
- We do not transmit or store audio recordings on permanent remote cloud databases.
- All voicemail recordings, neural speech transcripts, caller contact metadata, and preferences are stored locally on your device in a sandboxed, encrypted database.
- Temporary telephony gateway relays are cleared immediately after successful delivery to your device.

## 2. Information Handled On-Device
- **Phone State and Call Screening:** Used strictly to detect incoming calls, deflect unanswered or busy calls to your voicemail greeting, and screen callers.
- **Audio Files:** Voice recordings are stored directly in your private internal application storage and never indexed by external search engines or cloud backups.
- **Contacts:** Read-only access to match incoming caller phone numbers with names stored in your local address book. Your contacts list is never uploaded or shared.
- **Diagnostics:** Local call screening decision trail logs are stored on-device and can only be exported with your explicit consent.

## 3. Compliance with Applicable Privacy Frameworks
Depending on your selected region, {{APP_NAME}} adheres to applicable data protection legislation including {{APPLICABLE_PRIVACY_LAW}}:
- **Right to Access & Portability:** You have full access to view, listen to, and export all your voicemail data and transcripts at any time using the built-in Local Export feature.
- **Right to Immediate Erasure:** You can permanently delete individual voicemails or execute a complete local wipe via Settings > "Delete All Voicemails".
- **Purpose Limitation:** All granted telephony and microphone permissions are utilized strictly for call screening, greeting recording, and visual voicemail functions.

## 4. On-Device Security & Privacy Safeguards
- **Screen Capture Protection:** Optional screen capture blocking prevents screenshots or screen recordings of confidential transcripts by third-party applications.
- **Data Minimization:** No advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics trackers are integrated into {{APP_NAME}}.
