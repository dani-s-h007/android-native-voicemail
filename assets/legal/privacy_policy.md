# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 2026  
**Product:** Vani Voicemail  
**Developer:** Danish K ([https://danishk.web.app](https://danishk.web.app))

## 1. Zero Permanent Cloud Storage Commitment
Vani Voicemail operates under a strict **Zero-Cloud Data Policy**. We believe your private phone calls, voice recordings, and conversations belong exclusively to you.
- We do not operate permanent cloud storage servers for user voicemails.
- All audio recordings, transcripts, caller metadata, and preference files are encrypted and stored solely on your physical mobile device in a sandboxed local database (`vani_voicemail_local.db`).

## 2. Information Handled by the App
- **Phone State and Call Screening:** To identify incoming callers and invoke the assistant before your phone rings.
- **Audio Files:** Temporary audio downloads from telephony gateways are saved directly to your phone's internal storage and removed from the relay gateway.
- **Contacts:** Read-only access to match incoming caller phone numbers with names stored in your local address book. Contacts are never synced or exported.

## 3. Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act Compliance
In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023:
- **Right to Access & Correction:** You have direct access to view, edit, or search all your voicemail data directly within the app.
- **Right to Erasure:** Tapping "Delete All Voicemails" in Settings instantly and irrevocably purges all SQLite records and local audio files from your device.
- **Purpose Limitation:** Telephony permissions are used exclusively to process incoming calls and provide visual voicemail.

## 4. Security & On-Device Safeguards
- Optional **Screen Capture Protection** prevents other apps from taking screenshots of your private transcripts.
- Strict ProGuard/R8 rules strip debugging symbols and unauthorized reflection.
