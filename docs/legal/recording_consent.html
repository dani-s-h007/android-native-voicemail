Call Recording & Consent Notice

Document: Telephony Consent Architecture • Last Updated: November 2026 • Architect: Danish K (danishk.web.app)

1. The Mandatory Prior Notice Standard

In accordance with Indian telecommunications privacy guidelines and Section 5 of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, automated voice recording requires clear and unambiguous prior notice before audio capture initiates.

Standard System Disclosure Audio:
“Please leave a message after the beep. This message will be recorded for the recipient.”

2. Dynamic DND Custom Greetings

When the recipient configures a custom Do Not Disturb (DND) greeting (e.g., "I'm in a conference until 4 PM..."), the relay telephony dialplan is strictly programmed to append the recording consent notice immediately prior to the audible 440 Hz beep tone. The caller is thereby guaranteed an affirmative choice to disconnect before audio recording begins.

3. Caller Rights & Protections

  • Right to Disconnect: Callers who do not wish to have their message recorded may simply hang up before or immediately upon hearing the beep. Calls lasting less than 2 seconds are classified as ghost calls and shredded instantaneously.
  • No Cloud Retention: Callers' voices are never retained on cloud storage or used for commercial voice model training. Audio is routed to the intended recipient's device and erased from server RAM.
  • No Biometric Voice Profiling: Visual Voicemail Assistant performs no biometric voice extraction, voice-matching, or speaker identification on callers.
