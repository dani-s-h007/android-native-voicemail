2. Architectural Principle: 100% On-Device Sovereignty

Visual Voicemail Assistant adheres strictly to the principle of Data Minimization under Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023:

  • No Cloud Retention: Voice recordings and speech transcripts are never stored in any persistent cloud database.
  • Volatile Ephemeral Relay (RAM Only): When an unanswered or screened call is forwarded to the Indian relay virtual number, the server answers, records the audio into volatile memory (tmpfs RAM disk), and dispatches a high-priority push to your handset.
  • Cryptographic Shredding upon Delivery: The moment your handset downloads the audio file over HTTPS using a single-use signed URL and returns an acknowledgment (ACK), the relay server immediately overwrites and permanently destroys the RAM recording.
  • 24-Hour Auto-Purge: If your handset is offline, the uncollected audio file in volatile RAM is automatically purged after 24 hours.

4. Caller Notice & Recording Consent

Under Indian telecommunications and privacy standards, callers leaving a voicemail receive prior notice before audio capture begins:

“Please leave a message after the beep. This message will be recorded for the recipient.”

If the user activates Do Not Disturb (DND) mode with a customized text-to-speech message, the caller hears the user’s custom message followed by the statutory recording disclaimer and a beep tone.

5. Rights of the Data Principal (User Rights)

In accordance with Chapter III of the DPDP Act 2023, you retain comprehensive sovereignty over your personal data:

  • Right to Erasure: You can delete any individual voicemail, wipe all voicemails with a single tap, or enable automated auto-purge (7, 14, 30, or 90 days) in app settings.
  • Right to Export & Data Portability: You can export audio recordings as local WAV files or share them to external storage at any time.
  • Right to Revoke: Deactivating carrier forwarding (##002# or *413) terminates all call routing immediately.
