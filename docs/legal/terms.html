2. Service Description & Operation

The Application is a visual voicemail and call-assistant client designed for Android smartphones operating on telecommunications networks within India. The service functions by utilizing standard cellular supplementary service call-forwarding codes (MMI/USSD) to redirect unanswered, busy, or unreachable calls to a dedicated virtual relay number. Recorded voicemails are held temporarily in volatile memory (RAM) and transferred directly to your device's private storage, after which the relay server immediately deletes the recording.
